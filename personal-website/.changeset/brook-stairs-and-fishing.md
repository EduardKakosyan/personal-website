Rebuild the brook from Eduard's photo: shallow, clear water over red-brown stones, mossy rocks, earth banks, alders and birches leaning over it and a fallen birch. Add the stairs down to the water, the landing by the fishing hole and the tap where you get water; the guest can walk down, turn on the tap, and cast a line at the hole (cast, wait for the bite, strike, reel it in). HUGO leads the guest from the woodshed to the brook, the light turns golden by the woodshed and to sunset at the brook, and the brook has a lower camera view of its own.
