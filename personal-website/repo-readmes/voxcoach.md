# VoxCoach

## What

Sales-call practice with a simulated buyer and a review afterwards.

## Why

I explored a private, local voice loop for practicing conversations rather than recording real sales calls.

## How it works

The approved registry describes Go, local speech recognition, voice-activity detection, Qwen through Ollama and Kokoro synthesis. Generation overlaps speech, with barge-in and post-call review.
