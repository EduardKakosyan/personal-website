The fire at night (scene 7): once the guest has been to the brook, HUGO calls them back, night falls on the walk across the field, Eduard sits by the fire, and Rusty settles beside it with the cow. Sitting down in the camp chair brings up six questions for Eduard (each answer marked as a draft until he has written it himself); getting up afterwards is the goodbye, and Résumé and Contact light up in the corner bar. The cabin's string lights, window, lanterns and deck lights come on after dark, and there's a Camp Yahtzee set to find on the picnic table. `?night=1` opens straight at the fire.
